## 🗺️ Master Chronological Roadmap

```
Pre-History (300,000 BC – 2,500 BC)
│   ├── Palaeolithic Age: Lower, Middle, Upper (Bhimbetka Caves)
│   ├── Mesolithic Age: Microliths & Early Domestication (Bagor, Adamgarh)
│   └── Neolithic Age: Polished Stone Tools & Agriculture (Mehrgarh, Burzahom)
│
Proto-History (2,500 BC – 600 BC)
│   ├── Indus Valley Civilisation (2,500 BC – 1,750 BC): Urban grid, Harappa, Mohenjo-daro, Dholavira
│   ├── Early Vedic Period (1,500 BC – 1,000 BC): Pastoral economy, Rigveda, Jana polity
│   └── Later Vedic Period (1,000 BC – 600 BC): Iron Age (PGW), Janapadas, Varna system
│
Historical Period (600 BC – 750 AD)
│   ├── Age of Mahajanapadas & Heterodox Sects (600 BC – 321 BC): Magadha rise, Buddha & Mahavira
│   ├── Mauryan Empire (321 BC – 185 BC): Chandragupta, Ashoka, Centralized Administration, Dhamma
│   ├── Post-Mauryan Era (185 BC – 300 AD): Indo-Greeks, Shakas, Kushanas, Satavahanas, Sangam Age
│   ├── Gupta Empire (300 AD – 550 AD): Samudragupta, Chandragupta II, Classical Sanskrit Literature & Art
│   └── Post-Gupta & Early Medieval (550 AD – 1200 AD): Vardhanas (Harsha), Imperial Cholas, Rajputs
```
