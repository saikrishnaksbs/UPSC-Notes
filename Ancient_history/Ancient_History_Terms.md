### 1. Prehistoric & Protohistoric Cultures (IVC)

| Term | Historical Meaning & UPSC Context | Period / Domain |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **Microlith** | Miniature stone tools (1–5 cm) characteristic of Mesolithic hunting-gathering culture. | Mesolithic Culture |
| **Celt** | Polished, ground stone axe head defining Neolithic sedentary farming toolkits. | Neolithic Culture |
| **Citadel** | Raised western acropolis in Harappan cities housing public structures and elite residences. | Harappan Urbanism |
| **Lower Town** | Grid-planned eastern sector in IVC cities inhabited by ordinary artisans and citizens. | Harappan Urbanism |
| **Great Bath** | Waterproofed ceremonial public bath at Mohenjo-daro lined with bitumen and burnt bricks. | Harappan Architecture |
| **Dockyard** | Tidal stone basin at Lothal (Gujarat) for berthing seafaring merchant vessels. | Harappan Maritime Trade |
| **Steatite** | Soft magnesium-rich soapstone used to carve Harappan seals, beads, and figurines. | Harappan Crafts |
| **Cire Perdue** | Lost-wax metal casting technique used to manufacture the bronze *Dancing Girl* of Mohenjo-daro. | Harappan Metallurgy |
| **Pashupati Seal** | Famous steatite seal from Mohenjo-daro depicting a proto-Shiva yogic figure flanked by animals. | Harappan Religion |
| **Trepanation** | Prehistoric surgical skull boring observed in skeletal remains at Kalibangan and Burzahom. | Ancient Medicine |

### 2. Vedic Polity, Society & Economy

| Term | Historical Meaning & UPSC Context | Period / Domain |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **Rajan** | Tribal chieftain or king in Early Vedic society; protector of cattle and tribe (*Gopati*). | Rig Vedic Polity |
| **Sabha** | Council of select elders and Brahmin elites exercising judicial and political checks on king. | Vedic Polity |
| **Samiti** | General assembly of the entire Vedic tribe (*Vis*) electing the king and debating policy. | Vedic Polity |
| **Vidatha** | Oldest tribal folk assembly participating in secular, religious, and war-loot redistribution functions. | Rig Vedic Polity |
| **Gana / Vrata** | Tribal fighting units and autonomous community assemblies in Vedic society. | Vedic Society |
| **Purohita** | Chief royal priest and counselor directing imperial state rituals and battlefield prayers. | Vedic Administration |
| **Senani** | Commander-in-chief of tribal armed forces in the Rig Vedic age. | Vedic Administration |
| **Spasa** | Secret royal spies and intelligence messengers operating in Rig Vedic society. | Vedic Polity |
| **Vrajapati** | Officer in charge of pasture lands and head of fighting family units (*Kulas*). | Vedic Administration |
| **Gramani** | Headman of the village (*Grama*); leader of rural militia and administrative units. | Vedic Administration |
| **Kula** | Basic patriarchal family unit in Vedic society headed by the family elder (*Kulapa*). | Vedic Society |
| **Gavishti / Gaveshana**| 'Search for cows'; metaphorical term for inter-tribal battles in pastoral Vedic times. | Vedic Economy |
| **Bali** | Voluntary tribal tribute in Early Vedic times; transformed into compulsory tax in Later Vedic era. | Vedic Taxation |
| **Bhaga** | Sovereign royal share of agricultural produce (usually 1/6th) in Later Vedic times. | Vedic Taxation |
| **Sulka** | Customary duty or toll levied on traded merchandise and market goods. | Vedic Revenue |
| **Ayas** | Copper or bronze in Rig Veda; differentiated as *Syamayas* (iron) in Later Vedic texts. | Vedic Technology |
| **Nishka / Satamana**| Early gold and silver bullion ornaments and metal weights used as currency precursors. | Vedic Trade |
| **Rta** | Supreme cosmic, moral, and physical order of the universe upheld by god Varuna. | Vedic Philosophy |
| **Ashvamedha** | Horse sacrifice performed by imperial kings to assert undisputed sovereign paramountcy. | Vedic Rituals |
| **Rajasuya** | Royal consecration sacrifice conferring divine kingship status upon the monarch. | Vedic Rituals |

|:---|:---|:---|
| **Tirthankara** | 'Ford-maker'; one of the 24 liberated omniscient spiritual teachers of Jainism. | Jainism |
| **Kaivalya** | Supreme, infinite spiritual omniscience and liberation from karmic matter in Jain doctrine. | Jain Philosophy |
| **Triratna (Jain)** | The Three Jewels of Jainism: Right Faith (*Samyag Darshana*), Right Knowledge (*Samyag Jnana*), Right Conduct (*Samyag Charitra*). | Jain Ethics |
| **Anekantavada** | Jain metaphysical doctrine of multi-sidedness and plurality of reality and truth. | Jain Philosophy |
| **Syadvada** | Theory of conditional predication ('maybe/perhaps') expressing limitations of human judgment. | Jain Epistemology |
| **Sallekhana / Santhara**| Jain practice of voluntary religious submission to death through gradual fasting. | Jain Practices |
| **Dharmachakrapravartana**| 'Turning of the Wheel of Law'; Buddha's first sermon at Deer Park in Sarnath. | Buddhism |
| **Mahaparinirvana** | Final passing away and absolute release of the Buddha from the cycle of rebirth at Kushinagar. | Buddhism |
| **Sramana** | Wandering ascetic mendicant who rejected orthodox Vedic ritualism and social hierarchies. | Heterodox Movements |
| **Sangha** | Democratic monastic order of Buddhist monks (*Bhikkhus*) and nuns (*Bhikkhunis*). | Buddhist Institutions |
| **Upasaka / Upasika**| Lay male and female Buddhist followers who observed precepts without joining the monastery. | Buddhism |
| **Bodhisattva** | Enlightened being in Mahayana Buddhism who defers personal Nirvana to liberate all beings. | Mahayana Buddhism |
| **Arhat** | 'Worthy one'; perfected saint who has extinguished all defilements in Theravada Buddhism. | Theravada Buddhism |
| **Hinayana / Theravada**| 'Lesser Vehicle' / Way of Elders; orthodox early school emphasizing self-attained monastic Nirvana. | Buddhist Sects |
| **Mahayana** | 'Greater Vehicle'; devotional school worshipping Buddha as deity and venerating Bodhisattvas. | Buddhist Sects |
| **Vajrayana** | Tantric Buddhist vehicle relying on mantras, mandalas, and esoteric rituals for rapid enlightenment. | Buddhist Sects |

| **Navadhyaksha** | Superintendent of maritime navigation, river ferries, toll ports, and coastal shipping. | Mauryan Maritime |

| **Yuktas** | Subordinate clerical and accounting officials assisting Rajukas in district governance. | Mauryan Administration |
| **Dhamma Mahamattas**| Special moral officers instituted by Ashoka (14th regnal year) to propagate Dhamma. | Mauryan Governance |
| **Gopa** | Mauryan accountant supervising clusters of 5 to 10 rural villages. | Mauryan Administration |
| **Sthanika** | District officer administering one of the four quadrants of a Mauryan administrative province. | Mauryan Administration |
| **Vishti** | Unpaid forced labor exacted by the state from poor cultivators, artisans, and transport workers. | Mauryan Economy |
| **Vrajabhumi** | Officers appointed by Ashoka to look after pastures, cattle herds, and rural women's welfare. | Mauryan Administration |

### 5. Post-Mauryan & Sangam Age (Economy, Trade & Polity)

| Term | Historical Meaning & UPSC Context | Period / Domain |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **Sreni** | Merchant, artisan, or craftsman guild regulating product quality, prices, wages, and technical training. | Ancient Economy |
| **Nigama** | Urban merchant guild or town corporation functioning as commercial self-governing council. | Ancient Economy |
| **Puga** | Guild-like association of traders and craftsmen belonging to diverse castes but resident in one town. | Ancient Economy |
| **Sarthavaha** | Caravan leader directing overland long-distance merchant expeditions across trade routes. | Ancient Commerce |
| **Arikamedu** | Premier Indo-Roman coastal port emporium on Coromandel coast near Puducherry (*Poduke*). | Indo-Roman Trade |
| **Muziris** | Major ancient port on Malabar coast (Kerala) handling vast Roman gold and black pepper exports. | Ancient Maritime Trade |
| **Kurinji** | Sangam ecological zone (*Tinais*): Hilly/mountainous tract associated with hunting and honey gathering. | Sangam Geography |
| **Mullai** | Pastoral forest land in Sangam landscape associated with cattle rearing and dairy production. | Sangam Geography |
| **Marudam** | Fertile riverine agricultural plains in Sangam landscape associated with settled wet-paddy farming. | Sangam Geography |
| **Neithal** | Coastal littoral zone in Sangam landscape associated with fishing and salt manufacturing. | Sangam Geography |
| **Palai** | Arid/desert wilderness in Sangam landscape associated with banditry and warrior life. | Sangam Geography |
| **Enadi** | Military commander title conferred on distinguished captains by Tamil Sangam monarchs. | Sangam Polity |
| **Vellalar** | Dominant landed aristocracy and prosperous agricultural cultivating class in ancient Tamil country. | Sangam Society |
| **Kadaisiyar** | Landless agricultural laborers and marginalized rural day-workers in Sangam society. | Sangam Society |
| **Tolkappiyam** | Earliest extant Tamil grammatical, poetic, and sociological treatise authored by Tolkappiyar. | Sangam Literature |

### 6. Gupta & Post-Gupta Administration, Land Grants & Feudalism

| Term | Historical Meaning & UPSC Context | Period / Domain |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **Maharajadhiraja** | 'Great King of Kings'; supreme imperial title assumed by sovereign Gupta emperors. | Gupta Polity |
| **Uparika** | Provincial governor appointed by the Gupta emperor to administer a *Bhukti* (province). | Gupta Administration |
| **Vishayapati** | District administrator governing a *Vishaya* (district) under the provincial governor. | Gupta Administration |
| **Kumaramatya** | High-ranking cadre of imperial civil servants from which ministers, generals, and envoys were drawn. | Gupta Administration |
| **Sandhivigrahika** | Minister for peace and war (foreign affairs); post held by Harishena under Samudragupta. | Gupta Administration |
| **Mahabaladhikrita** | Commander-in-chief of imperial armed forces during the Gupta Empire. | Gupta Military |
| **Pustapala** | Official record-keeper maintaining land registers and boundary charters in Gupta districts. | Gupta Administration |
| **Agrahara** | Perpetual tax-exempt royal land grant gifted to Brahmanas along with all revenue and administrative rights. | Land Systems |
| **Brahmadeya** | Tax-free land grant gifted specifically to individual Brahmin scholars or scholarly communities. | Land Systems |
| **Devadana** | Land granted tax-free to Hindu temples or monastic institutions for spiritual maintenance. | Land Systems |
| **Kshetra** | Cultivated agricultural land suitable for regular seasonal crop production. | Gupta Agriculture |
| **Khila** | Uncultivated fallow land or barren waste brought under cultivation through royal grants. | Gupta Agriculture |
| **Aprahata** | Forested or uncultivated jungle land untouched by previous settlement or plows. | Gupta Agriculture |
| **Vasti** | Land designated and used specifically for residential settlement and housing. | Gupta Land Types |
| **Dinara** | High-purity imperial gold coin issued by Gupta monarchs, inspired by Roman *denarius*. | Gupta Currency |
| **Kayastha** | Emergent professional sub-caste (*Jati*) of royal scribes, draftsmen, and revenue accountants. | Gupta Society |
| **Samanta** | Feudal tributary chief or vassal subordinate to imperial overlord; hallmark of Indian feudalism. | Indian Feudalism |

### 7. South Indian Dynasties (Cholas, Pallavas, Chalukyas)

| Term | Historical Meaning & UPSC Context | Period / Domain |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **Ur** | General village assembly of tax-paying non-Brahmin landholders in Chola rural administration. | Chola Administration |
| **Sabha / Mahasabha**| Autonomous gathering of Brahmin landholders in *Agrahara* / *Brahmadeya* villages. | Chola Administration |
| **Nagaram** | Assembly of merchants and commercial traders managing local market and urban municipal affairs. | Chola Commerce |
| **Kudavolai** | Lottery ballot system using palm-leaf tickets drawn from pots to elect Chola *Variyam* committee members. | Chola Local Self-Govt |
| **Variyam** | Executive committees of the Chola village Sabha managing tanks (*Eri*), gardens (*Tottam*), and justice. | Chola Administration |
| **Taniyur** | Distinct autonomous revenue unit or independent large village separate from ordinary Nadu districts. | Chola Administration |
| **Eripatti** | Special village land set aside whose revenues were strictly reserved for repairing village irrigation tanks. | Chola Irrigation |
| **Kadamai** | Basic land revenue tax paid in paddy produce to the Chola royal government. | Chola Revenue |
| **Vetti** | Forced unpaid labor extracted by the state for digging irrigation canals and building public temples. | Chola Revenue |
| **Devadasi** | Temple dancing women dedicated to deity worship, preserving classical temple arts and Bharatanatyam. | South Indian Society |
| **Ghatika** | Ancient center of advanced higher learning and Brahmanical Vedic studies attached to temples. | Pallava Education |

### 8. Ancient Architecture & Cultural Terms

| Term | Historical Meaning & UPSC Context | Period / Domain |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **Chaitya** | Buddhist apsidal rock-cut or structural assembly hall containing a stupa at rear for congregational worship. | Buddhist Architecture |
| **Vihara** | Buddhist rock-cut or brick residential monastery for monks and nuns (*Sangharama*). | Buddhist Architecture |
| **Stupa** | Hemispherical commemorative earthen/stone tumulus enshrining relics of Buddha or Buddhist saints. | Buddhist Architecture |
| **Torana** | Ceremonial carved gateway standing at four cardinal entrances to a Buddhist stupa railing. | Buddhist Architecture |
| **Harmika** | Square railed balcony situated atop the hemispherical dome (*Anda*) of a stupa. | Buddhist Architecture |
| **Chhatra** | Triple-tiered umbrella parasol crowning a stupa representing Buddha, Dhamma, and Sangha. | Buddhist Architecture |
| **Ayagapatas** | Jain stone ornamental tablets of homage and worship decorated with auspicious symbols (*Ashtamangala*). | Jain Art |
| **Shikhara** | Curvilinear or pyramidal superstructure crowning the sanctum of a Hindu temple. | Temple Architecture |
| **Garbhagriha** | 'Womb-chamber'; unadorned inner sanctum of a temple housing the primary presiding deity. | Temple Architecture |
| **Mandapa** | Pillared porch or congregational hall leading into the temple sanctum. | Temple Architecture |
| **Antarala** | Vestibule or transitional antechamber connecting the Garbhagriha to the Mandapa. | Temple Architecture |
| **Panchayatana** | Temple architectural layout where primary central shrine is surrounded by four subsidiary corner shrines. | Temple Architecture |
