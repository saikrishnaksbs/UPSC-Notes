| Rock cut temples at Mahabalipuram | Pallava rulers of Kanchipuram: Narasimhavarman; 7 cen AD | Consists of Rathas named after Pandavas, Draupadi, Dharamraja and Ganesha. |
| Shore temple at Mahabalipuram | Narasimhavarman: Pallava Ruler; 7 cen A. D | Outside walls consists of seated bull figurines. |
| Kailasanath temple at Kanchipuram | Raja Simha: Pallava Ruler; 8cen AD | Consists of Garbhagriha, Mandapa and ambulatory. |

| Lingaraja temple, Bhubaneshwar | Somavamsi and Ganga rulers; 11 century AD | It is dedicated to Lord Shiva. |

| Kailasa temple, Ellora | Krishna 1: Rashtrakuta ruler; 8 century AD | It is a mix of Pallava and Chalukyan architecture. |
| Khajuraho, Madhya Pradesh | Chandellas; 900-1130 AD | Consists of Hindu and Jain temples. |
| Virupaksha temple, Hampi | Vijayanagar ruler; 7 century AD | It is dedicated to Shiva and is an imitation of Kailash Nath, Kanchipuram. |
| Lad Khan temple, Aihole | Chalukyan rulers; 5 cen AD | It is one of the oldest temples and dedicated to lord Shiva. |
