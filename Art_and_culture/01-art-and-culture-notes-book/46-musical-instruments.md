# Musical Instruments

> **Source**: [Musical Instruments](https://compass.rauias.com/art-culture/musical-instruments/)

---

- In Natyashastra musical instruments have been divided into **four main categoriesbased on how sound is produced. These include **Stringed, wind, percussion and solid instruments.**

- **Santoora stringed instrument is popular in Kashmir.

- **Kamaicha **is a bowed lute played by Manganiars of west Rajasthan.

- **Shehnaiis a reed instrument and its use is known as Mangal Vidya. Ustad Bismillah Khan is credited for popularizing this instrument.

- **Tablais a set of two vertical Oordhwaka drums. The right side is called tabla and left is Bayan or Dagga. Prominent musicians are Ustad Alla Rakha Khan, Zakir Hussain etc.

- In Sun temple, Konark a large sculpture of a female Jhanj player, of 8 ft. is found.
