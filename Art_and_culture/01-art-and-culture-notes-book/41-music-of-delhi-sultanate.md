# Music of Delhi Sultanate

> **Source**: [Music of Delhi Sultanate](https://compass.rauias.com/art-culture/music-delhi-sultanate/)

---

- Turks had inherited a rich musical tradition from Arabs. New musical instruments such as **rabab and sarangiwere introduced. Sufism and Bhakti movement further gave impetus to the musical style.

- **Amir Khusrau and Sufi Saint Pir Bodhan **are pioneers of this phase.

- Coming of Turks to India brought **Perso-Arabic airs (ragas),such as aiman, ghora, sanam etc. Indian classical work on music **Raga Darpanwas translated to Persian during the **reign of Firuz Tughlaq**.

- **Raja Man Singh of Gwaliorwas a great music lover (**Man Kautuhalof his reign included musical nodes of Muslims).
