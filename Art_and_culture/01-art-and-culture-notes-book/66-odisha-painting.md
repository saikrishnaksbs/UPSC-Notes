# Odisha Painting

> **Source**: [Odisha Painting](https://compass.rauias.com/art-culture/odisha-painting/)

---

- The earliest surviving examples of miniature painting in Odisha appear in 17th century AD.

- Examples: Manuscripts of Gita Govinda an illustrated palm ­leaf manuscript of the Ramayana.

- In Odisha, palm-leaf continued to be used up to the 19th century.
