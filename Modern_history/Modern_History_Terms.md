### British Expansion

| Term | Historical Meaning & UPSC Context | Period / Exam Domain |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Subsidiary Alliance** | Policy of Lord Wellesley where Indian rulers surrendered foreign relations, hosted British troops, and accepted a Resident. | British Expansion |
| **Doctrine of Lapse** | Policy of Lord Dalhousie annexing princely states whose rulers died without a natural male heir (Satara, Jhansi, Nagpur). | British Expansion |

### British Land Policy

| Term | Historical Meaning & UPSC Context | Period / Exam Domain |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Permanent Settlement** | Land revenue system introduced by Lord Cornwallis in Bengal (1793) creating hereditary Zamindars with fixed revenue demands. | British Land Policy |
| **Ryotwari System** | Direct land settlement with individual cultivators (Ryots) introduced by Thomas Munro in Madras and Bombay (1820). | British Land Policy |
| **Mahalwari System** | Land revenue settlement made with village communities (Mahals) introduced by Holt Mackenzie (1822) in NWFP and Punjab. | British Land Policy |
| **Sunset Law** | Clause under Permanent Settlement specifying that if revenue was not paid by sunset of the fixed date, the Zamindari was auctioned. | British Land Policy |

### Freedom Struggle

| Term | Historical Meaning & UPSC Context | Period / Exam Domain |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Swadeshi** | Movement advocating boycott of foreign goods and promotion of indigenous industries (1905 anti-Partition movement). | Freedom Struggle |
| **Non-Cooperation Movement** | Mass movement (1920-22) surrendering titles, boycotting schools, courts, and foreign goods after Jallianwala Bagh. | Freedom Struggle |
| **Civil Disobedience Movement** | Mass movement (1930-34) initiated with Dandi Salt March breaking salt tax law. | Freedom Struggle |
| **Quit India Movement** | Mass uprising (1942) launched after Gandhiji's "Do or Die" call demanding immediate British withdrawal. | Freedom Struggle |
| **Home Rule League** | Movement launched by Tilak and Annie Besant (1916) demanding self-government within the British Empire. | Freedom Struggle |

### Revolutionary Struggle

| Term | Historical Meaning & UPSC Context | Period / Exam Domain |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Ghadar Movement** | Transnational revolutionary organization formed by Indian immigrants in San Francisco (1913) led by Lala Har Dayal. | Revolutionary Struggle |
| **Anushilan Samiti** | Revolutionary secret society formed in Bengal (1902) advocating armed overthrow of British rule. | Revolutionary Struggle |
| **Hindustan Socialist Republican Association (HSRA)** | Revolutionary organization (1928) led by Bhagat Singh, Chandrashekhar Azad, and Sukhdev advocating socialism and armed revolution. | Revolutionary Struggle |
