# Medieval Indian History: Master Chronology (712 CE to 1761 CE)

> **UPSC Revision Tool**: Chronological sequencing of all early medieval, sultanate, and imperial Mughal milestones.
> **Cross-Reference**: Aligned with , Satish Chandra, and Poonam Dalal Dahiya.

---

## 🏛️ Comprehensive Medieval Chronological Master Table

| Era / Date | Dynastic Epoch | Landmark Historical Event | Key Personalities / Sources | UPSC Key Takeaway & Significance |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **712 CE** | Arab Invasion | Muhammad bin Qasim captures Sindh | Raja Dahir of Sindh, Chachnama | Earliest Arab foothold in India; introduction of *Jizya* and date-palm cultivation. |
| **c. 750 – 900 CE** | Tripartite Struggle | Conflict for control of imperial Kanauj | Palas (Dharmapala), Pratiharas (Vatsaraja), Rashtrakutas (Dhruva) | Kanauj as symbol of northern sovereignty; emergence of pan-regional feudatories. |
| **985 – 1014 CE** | Imperial Cholas | Rajaraja Chola I expands southern empire | Brihadisvara Temple (Thanjavur) | Local village self-government (*Kudavolai* system in Uttaramerur inscription); naval control of Maldives. |
| **1014 – 1044 CE** | Imperial Cholas | Rajendra Chola I maritime expeditions | Gangaikonda Cholapuram, Srivijaya raid | Naval expedition across Bay of Bengal; defeated Mahipala I of Bengal (assumed title *Gangaikonda*). |
| **1000 – 1027 CE** | Ghaznavid Incursions | Mahmud of Ghazni's 17 raiding expeditions | Al-Biruni (*Tahqiq-i-Hind*), Firdausi | Plunder of Nagarkot, Thanesar, Mathura, Kanauj; sack of Somnath Temple (1025 CE). |
| **1191 CE** | Rajput Resistance | First Battle of Tarain | Prithviraj Chauhan III vs. Muhammad Ghori | Decisive victory for Rajput confederacy; Ghori routed and wounded. |
| **1192 CE** | Turkish Conquest | Second Battle of Tarain | Muhammad Ghori defeats Prithviraj Chauhan | Decisive turning point establishing Turkish Muslim dominion across North India. |
| **1194 CE** | Ghurid Expansion | Battle of Chandawar | Muhammad Ghori defeats King Jaichandra | Annexation of Gahadavala kingdom of Kanauj and Varanasi. |
| **1206 CE** | Delhi Sultanate (Slave) | Qutbuddin Aibak establishes Sultanate | Qutbuddin Aibak (*Lakhbaksh*) | Independent Sultanate of Delhi founded; started construction of Qutb Minar. |
| **1210 – 1236 CE** | Delhi Sultanate (Slave) | Consolidation under Shamsuddin Iltutmish | Silver *Tanka*, Copper *Jital*, Iqta System | Real consolidator of Sultanate; organized the Turkan-i-Chahalgani (Group of Forty). |
| **1236 – 1240 CE** | Delhi Sultanate (Slave) | Reign of Raziya Sultan | Minhaj-i-Siraj (*Tabaqat-i-Nasiri*) | First and only sovereign Muslim woman ruler of Delhi; resisted noble orthodox bigotry. |
| **1266 – 1287 CE** | Delhi Sultanate (Slave) | Balban's Autocratic Kingship | Ghiyasuddin Balban, Amir Khusrau | Policy of *Blood and Iron*; theory of *Niyabat-i-Khudai*; introduced *Sijdah*, *Paibos*, and *Nauroz*. |
| **1290 CE** | Delhi Sultanate (Khalji) | Khalji Revolution; end of Ilbari monopoly | Jalaluddin Khalji | Broadened social base of ruling elite beyond orthodox Turkish monopoly. |
| **1296 – 1316 CE** | Delhi Sultanate (Khalji) | Imperial expansion and economic market reforms | Alauddin Khalji, Malik Kafur, Amir Khusrau | Price controls, *Shahna-i-Mandi*, *Dagh* and *Chehra*, land measurement (*Biswa*); Chittor siege (1303). |
| **1320 CE** | Delhi Sultanate (Tughlaq) | Ghiyasuddin Tughlaq founds Tughlaq dynasty | Ghazi Malik, Amir Khusrau (*Tughlaqnama*) | Built Tughlaqabad Fort; established direct royal control over Bengal and Deccan. |
| **1325 – 1351 CE** | Delhi Sultanate (Tughlaq) | Visionary experiments of Muhammad bin Tughlaq | Ibn Battuta (*Rihla*), Ziauddin Barani | Capital shift to Daulatabad (1327); Token currency (1329); agricultural department (*Diwan-i-Kohi*). |
| **1336 CE** | Vijayanagara Empire | Foundation of the Vijayanagara Empire | Harihara I & Bukka I (Sangama Dynasty) | Founded on Tungabhadra banks under guidance of sage Vidyaranya; resisted northern sultanates. |
| **1347 CE** | Bahmani Sultanate | Foundation of the Bahmani Kingdom | Alauddin Hasan Bahman Shah | Capital established at Gulbarga; first independent Muslim kingdom in Deccan. |
| **1351 – 1388 CE** | Delhi Sultanate (Tughlaq) | Welfare administration of Firoz Shah Tughlaq | Canals, *Diwan-i-Khairat*, *Bandagan* | Excavated major canals; separated Jizya from land revenue; established hospital (*Dar-ul-Shifa*). |
| **1398 CE** | Foreign Invasion | Amir Timur (Tamerlane) sacks Delhi | Timur, Sultan Mahmud Shah Tughlaq | Devastating plunder and massacre in Delhi; shattered the central prestige of Delhi Sultanate. |
| **1414 – 1451 CE** | Delhi Sultanate (Sayyid) | Rule of Sayyid Dynasty | Khizr Khan, Mubarak Shah, Yahya Sirhindi | Tributary to Timurids; preserved local authority in Delhi-Agra doab. |
| **1451 – 1526 CE** | Delhi Sultanate (Lodi) | Rule of the First Afghan Empire | Bahlul Lodi, Sikandar Lodi, Ibrahim Lodi | Sikandar founded Agra (1504), introduced *Gaz-i-Sikandari*; Ibrahim defeated at Panipat. |
| **1498 CE** | European Maritime Arrival | Vasco da Gama reaches Calicut (Kozhikode) | Zamorin of Calicut, Portuguese explorers | Opened direct Cape route from Europe to India, beginning European colonial era. |
| **1509 – 1529 CE** | Vijayanagara Empire | Golden Age of Krishnadevaraya (Tuluva) | Krishnadevaraya, Domingo Paes, Duarte Barbosa | Peak of Vijayanagara power; authored *Amuktamalyada*; built Hazara Rama and Vittala temples. |
| **1526 CE** | Mughal Empire (Early) | First Battle of Panipat | Babur defeats Ibrahim Lodi | Ended Delhi Sultanate; founded Mughal Empire; first use of gunpowder artillery and *Tulghuma*. |
| **1527 CE** | Mughal Empire (Early) | Battle of Khanwa | Babur defeats Rajput confederacy under Rana Sanga | Established Mughal military supremacy across northern India; Babur assumed title *Ghazi*. |
| **1528 – 1529 CE** | Mughal Empire (Early) | Battles of Chanderi & Ghaghra | Babur defeats Medini Rai & Afghan coalition | Consolidated Mughal control over Malwa, Bihar, and the Gangetic plains. |
| **1530 – 1540 CE** | Mughal Empire (Early) | Humayun's first reign & struggles with Afghans | Humayun, Sher Shah Sur (Farid) | Defeated at **Battle of Chausa (1539)** and **Battle of Bilgram/Kannauj (1540)**; fled to Persia. |
| **1540 – 1545 CE** | Sur Empire | Administrative reforms of Sher Shah Sur | Abbas Khan Sarwani (*Tarikh-i-Sher Shahi*) | Standardized silver *Rupiya*; rebuilt Grand Trunk Road (*Sadak-i-Azam*); *Patta* & *Qabuliyat*. |
| **1555 CE** | Mughal Restoration | Humayun reconquers Delhi and Agra | Humayun, Bairam Khan (Battle of Sirhind) | Restored Mughal sovereignty with Safavid Persian military support; died in accident (1556). |
| **1556 CE** | Mughal Empire (Akbar) | Second Battle of Panipat | 13-year-old Akbar & Bairam Khan defeat Hemu | Smashed Afghan military challenge; inaugurated half-century reign of Akbar the Great. |
| **1565 CE** | Deccan Warfare | Battle of Talikota (Rakkasagi-Tangadi) | Deccan Sultanate Coalition vs. Vijayanagara | Sacked and destroyed Hampi; shattered the military power of the Vijayanagara Empire. |
| **1568 – 1572 CE** | Mughal Expansion | Conquest of Chittor, Ranthambore, and Gujarat | Akbar, Maharana Udai Singh | Chittorgarh siege; captured Gujarat; laid foundation of imperial port trade at Surat. |
| **1571 CE** | Mughal Administration | Foundation of Fatehpur Sikri & Mansabdari | Akbar, Shaikh Salim Chishti | Universal civil-military grading system (*Zat* and *Sawar*); architectural synthesis. |
| **1575 CE** | Mughal Religious Dialogue | Establishment of the Ibadat Khana | Akbar, theologians of multiple faiths | Theological debating hall; led to realization of moral truth across all religious faiths. |
| **1576 CE** | Rajput Resistance | Battle of Haldighati & Annexation of Bengal | Raja Man Singh vs. Maharana Pratap | Maharana Pratap sustained guerrilla warfare in Aravallis; Daud Khan Karrani killed in Bengal. |
| **1579 CE** | Mughal Sovereignty | The Mahzar (Infallibility Decree) | Drafted by Sheikh Mubarak (Abul Fazl's father) | Declared Akbar *Imam-i-Adil* (supreme arbiter in religious interpretation). |
| **1580 CE** | Mughal Revenue System | Institution of Ain-i-Dahsala (Todar Mal's Bandobast) | Raja Todar Mal, Abul Fazl (*Ain-i-Akbari*) | 10-year average assessment of crop yields and prices; state revenue fixed in cash. |
| **1582 CE** | Mughal Ideology | Promulgation of Tauhid-i-Ilahi (Din-i-Ilahi) | Akbar, Birbal (sole prominent Hindu follower) | Ethical syncretic order emphasizing *Sulh-i-Kul* (universal peace and religious toleration). |
| **1605 – 1627 CE** | Mughal Empire (Jahangir)| Reign of Nuruddin Muhammad Jahangir | Jahangir, Nur Jahan, William Hawkins, Sir Thomas Roe | Golden age of Mughal portrait painting; *Zanjir-i-Adl* (Chain of Justice); execution of Guru Arjan (1606). |
| **1628 – 1658 CE** | Mughal Empire (Shah Jahan)| Architectural Golden Age of White Marble | Shah Jahan, Ustad Ahmad Lahori | Taj Mahal (1631–1648), Red Fort Delhi, Jama Masjid; Peacock Throne (*Takht-i-Taus*). |
| **1658 – 1707 CE** | Mughal Empire (Aurangzeb)| Reign of Muhi-ud-Din Muhammad Aurangzeb | Aurangzeb (Alamgir), Dara Shikoh | War of Succession (Samugarh 1658); Annexation of Bijapur (1686) & Golconda (1687); Jizya reimposed (1679). |
| **1674 CE** | Maratha Empire | Coronation of Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj | Shivaji, Gaga Bhatta (Raigad Fort) | Sovereign Maratha *Hindavi Swarajya* established; *Ashtapradhan* council; *Chauth* & *Sardeshmukhi*. |
| **1680 – 1707 CE** | Maratha War of Independence | 27-Year War against Mughal Occupation | Sambhaji, Rajaram, Maharani Tarabai, Santaji Ghorpade | Mughals captured and executed Sambhaji (1689); Marathas waged successful people's guerrilla war. |
| **1707 CE** | Mughal Collapse | Death of Aurangzeb at Ahmednagar | Aurangzeb, Prince Muazzam (Bahadur Shah I) | Exhaustion of imperial treasury; Jagirdari crisis; rapid disintegration under Later Mughals. |
| **1713 – 1719 CE** | Later Mughals | Dominance of the Sayyid Brothers (Kingmakers) | Abdullah Khan & Husain Ali Khan; Farrukhsiyar | Farrukhsiyar's 1717 Farman granted duty-free trade concessions to British East India Company. |
| **1720 – 1740 CE** | Maratha Ascendancy | Expansion under Peshwa Baji Rao I | Baji Rao I, Chhatrapati Shahu | Promoted Maratha Confederacy; conquered Malwa, Gujarat, Bundelkhand; defeated Nizam at Palkhed (1728). |
| **1739 CE** | Foreign Catastrophe | Nadir Shah of Persia sacks Delhi | Nadir Shah, Emperor Muhammad Shah Rangeela | Battle of Karnal; looted Peacock Throne, Koh-i-Noor diamond, and 70 crore rupees in bullion. |
| **1761 CE** | Pan-Indian Climax | Third Battle of Panipat | Ahmad Shah Abdali vs. Sadashivrao Bhau | Smashed Maratha imperial bid for Delhi; created strategic vacuum exploited by British East India Company. |
