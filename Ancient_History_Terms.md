### Architecture

| :--- | :--- | :--- |

### Buddhism

| Term | Historical Meaning & UPSC Context | Period / Exam Domain |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |

### Gupta Land System

| Term | Historical Meaning & UPSC Context | Period / Exam Domain |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |

| **Dronavapa** | Land measurement unit smaller than Kulyavapa used for seed-sowing ratio calculations. | Gupta Land System |

### Mauryan Administration

| Term | Historical Meaning & UPSC Context | Period / Exam Domain |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Mahamatras** | High-ranking officials appointed by Ashoka to spread Dhamma, supervise provincial administration, and oversee judicial/social affairs. | Mauryan Administration |

| **Sannidhata** | Chief Custodian of the State Treasury and Storehouses during the Mauryan period. | Mauryan Administration |
| **Yukta** | Subordinate revenue officer in Mauryan district administration responsible for revenue compilation. | Mauryan Administration |
| **Rajjuka** | District revenue and judicial officer appointed by Ashoka to measure land, assess revenue, and administer justice. | Mauryan Administration |
