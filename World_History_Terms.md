### Cold War

| Term | Historical Meaning & UPSC Context | Period / Exam Domain |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Iron Curtain** | Boundary dividing Europe into Western democratic and Eastern Soviet communist spheres, coined by Winston Churchill (1946). | Cold War |
| **Truman Doctrine** | US foreign policy (1947) pledging financial and military support to contain the spread of Soviet Communism. | Cold War |
| **Marshall Plan** | American economic aid program (1948) rebuilding war-torn Western European economies to prevent communist influence. | Cold War |

### French Revolution

| Term | Historical Meaning & UPSC Context | Period / Exam Domain |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |

| **Sans-culottes** | Radical working-class militants of the French Revolution named for wearing long trousers instead of aristocratic knee-breeches. | French Revolution |
