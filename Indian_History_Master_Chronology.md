## 🏛️ Comprehensive Three-Era Unified Master Timeline

| Era / Major Date | Broad Historical Period | Key Watershed Event & Monument / Text | Pivotal Historic Figures | Crucial UPSC Prelims/Mains Anchor |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **c. 2,000,000 – 10,000 BCE** | Paleolithic Age | Stone tool evolution: Pebble tools to blades | Early Hominins, Bhimbetka Rock Shelters | Hunting-gathering stage; emergence of rock art and burial rituals. |
| **c. 10,000 – 6,000 BCE** | Mesolithic Age | Microlithic technology; early domestication | Bagor, Adamgarh, Sarai Nahar Rai | Geometric microliths; transition from hunting to herding animals. |
| **c. 7,000 – 2,500 BCE** | Neolithic Revolution | Settled village farming, pottery, polished celts | Mehrgarh (Baluchistan), Burzahom, Chirand | Earliest wheat/barley agriculture; pit dwellings and bone tools. |
| **c. 2,600 – 1,900 BCE** | Indus Valley (IVC) | Peak Bronze Age urbanization & grid towns | Harappa, Mohenjo-daro, Dholavira, Lothal | Drainage systems, Great Bath, dockyard, granaries, steatite seals. |
| **c. 1,500 – 1,000 BCE** | Early Vedic Period | Pastoral tribal economy; Rig Veda hymns | Rig Vedic Aryans; Sapta Sindhu | Sabha and Samiti popular councils; cattle-centric pastoralism (*Gavishti*). |
| **c. 1,000 – 600 BCE** | Later Vedic Period | Iron Age; territorial states; Painted Grey Ware | Kuru-Panchala; Sama, Yajur, Atharva | Sedentary plow agriculture; Ashvamedha rituals; four-varna hierarchy. |
| **c. 600 – 500 BCE** | Second Urbanization | 16 Mahajanapadas; rise of Buddhism & Jainism | Gautama Buddha, Mahavira, Bimbisara | Magadha emergence; punch-marked coinage; heterodox sramana philosophies. |
| **c. 326 BCE** | Macedonian Invasion | Alexander the Great invades Punjab | Alexander, King Porus (Hydaspes) | Direct Greco-Indian cultural contacts; opening of overland trade routes. |
| **c. 322 – 185 BCE** | Mauryan Empire | First Pan-Indian imperial state | Chandragupta, Chanakya, Ashoka | Kautilya's Arthashastra; Rock & Pillar Edicts; Ashoka's Dhamma. |
| **c. 2nd Cent BCE – 3rd Cent CE**| Post-Mauryan Era | Central Asian migrations; Silk Route trade; Sangam | Indo-Greeks, Kushanas, Satavahanas | Shaka Era (78 CE); Gandhara & Mathura art; Roman trade (*Arikamedu*). |
| **c. 319 – 550 CE** | Imperial Guptas | Classical Golden Age; Sanskrit literature peak | Samudragupta, Chandragupta II (*Vikramaditya*) | Prayag Prashasti; Kalidasa's works; Ajanta murals; Nalanda founded. |
| **c. 606 – 647 CE** | Post-Gupta Era | Kanauj empire of Harsha; Deccan Chalukyas | Harsha Vardhana, Pulakeshin II, Xuanzang | Kanauj Assembly; Aihole inscription; Harsha-Pulakeshin Narmada battle. |
| **c. 750 – 900 CE** | Early Medieval Era | Tripartite Struggle for imperial Kanauj | Palas (Dharmapala), Pratiharas, Rashtrakutas | Proliferation of land grants (*Agrahara*); rise of regional feudatories. |
| **712 CE** | Arab Conquest | Muhammad bin Qasim captures Sindh | Raja Dahir of Sindh, Chachnama | Earliest Islamic foothold in subcontinent; introduction of Jizya. |
| **985 – 1044 CE** | Imperial Cholas | Chola maritime empire & local self-government | Rajaraja I, Rajendra I | Brihadisvara Temple; Kudavolai ballot system; Srivijaya naval expedition. |
| **1000 – 1027 CE** | Ghaznavid Raids | Mahmud of Ghazni's 17 raiding incursions | Mahmud, Al-Biruni (*Tahqiq-i-Hind*) | Sack of Somnath (1025 CE); extensive plunder of northern temple wealth. |
| **1192 CE** | Turkish Conquest | Second Battle of Tarain | Muhammad Ghori defeats Prithviraj Chauhan | Foundation of permanent Turkish Muslim dominion across Gangetic plains. |
| **1206 – 1290 CE** | Delhi Sultanate (Slave)| Mamluk Rule; consolidation of Delhi Sultanate | Qutbuddin Aibak, Iltutmish, Balban | Turkan-i-Chahalgani; silver Tanka & copper Jital; Policy of Blood & Iron. |
| **1296 – 1316 CE** | Delhi Sultanate (Khalji)| Imperial southern conquests & price control reforms | Alauddin Khalji, Malik Kafur, Amir Khusrau | Market control (*Shahna-i-Mandi*); Dagh & Chehra; land measurement. |
| **1325 – 1351 CE** | Delhi Sultanate (Tughlaq)| Bold administrative experiments of MBT | Muhammad bin Tughlaq, Ibn Battuta | Capital shift to Daulatabad; Token currency; Diwan-i-Kohi created. |
| **1336 – 1646 CE** | Southern Empire | Vijayanagara Empire | Harihara, Bukka, Krishnadevaraya | Nayankara & Ayagar systems; Hampi architecture; Amuktamalyada. |
| **1347 – 1527 CE** | Deccan Sultanate | Bahmani Kingdom & 5 Deccan Sultanates | Alauddin Bahman Shah, Mahmud Gawan | Mahmud Gawan's Madrasa at Bidar; Gol Gumbaz at Bijapur. |
| **1526 CE** | Mughal Dawn | First Battle of Panipat | Babur defeats Ibrahim Lodi | Gunpowder artillery & Tulghuma tactics; foundation of Mughal Empire. |
| **1540 – 1545 CE** | Sur Empire | Administrative reorganization under Sher Shah | Sher Shah Sur (*Farid Khan*) | Standardization of silver *Rupiya*; Grand Trunk Road; Patta & Qabuliyat. |
| **1556 – 1605 CE** | Mughal Zenith | Imperial consolidation under Akbar the Great | Akbar, Todar Mal, Bairam Khan, Birbal | Second Panipat (1556); Mansabdari System; Ain-i-Dahsala; Sulh-i-Kul. |
| **1628 – 1658 CE** | Mughal Architecture | Golden Age of White Marble Architecture | Shah Jahan, Ustad Ahmad Lahori | Taj Mahal, Red Fort Delhi, Jama Masjid; Peacock Throne. |
| **1674 – 1680 CE** | Maratha Swarajya | Coronation of Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj | Shivaji, Gaga Bhatta (Raigad Fort) | Ashtapradhan council; Chauth & Sardeshmukhi; Ganimi Kawa guerrilla war. |
| **1658 – 1707 CE** | Mughal Decline | Reign of Aurangzeb (Alamgir) | Aurangzeb, Guru Tegh Bahadur, Sambhaji | 27-year Deccan war; reimposition of Jizya; Jagirdari crisis. |
| **1739 CE** | Foreign Invasion | Nadir Shah of Persia sacks Delhi | Nadir Shah, Muhammad Shah Rangeela | Battle of Karnal; looting of Peacock Throne and Koh-i-Noor diamond. |
| **1757 (23 June)** | British Expansion | Battle of Plassey | Robert Clive, Siraj-ud-Daulah, Mir Jafar | Treason and conspiracy seal British territorial control over Bengal. |
| **1761 (14 Jan)** | Pan-Indian Climax | Third Battle of Panipat | Ahmad Shah Abdali vs. Sadashivrao Bhau | Smashed Maratha bid for Delhi; created power vacuum for British advance. |
| **1764 (22 Oct)** | British Conquest | Battle of Buxar | Major Hector Munro vs. Triple Indian Alliance | Definitive military victory; Treaty of Allahabad grants Diwani rights. |
| **1793** | Colonial Land Policy | Permanent Settlement of Bengal | Lord Cornwallis, Sir John Shore | Recognized Zamindars as permanent owners; fixed revenue; Sunset Law. |
| **1799** | Southern Supremacy | Fourth Anglo-Mysore War; death of Tipu Sultan | Tipu Sultan, Lord Wellesley | Fall of Seringapatam; elimination of French influence in southern India. |
| **1818** | Complete Paramountcy | Third Anglo-Maratha War | Lord Hastings vs. Peshwa Baji Rao II | Peshwaship abolished; complete Pax Britannica across Central India & Deccan. |
| **1849** | Frontier Annexation | Second Anglo-Sikh War; Annexation of Punjab | Lord Dalhousie, Sher Singh, Duleep Singh | British Indian frontier reaches Indus River and Khyber Pass. |
| **1857 (May)** | Anti-Colonial Conflagration| The Great Revolt of 1857 | Sepoys, Nana Saheb, Lakshmibai, Zafar | Outbreak at Meerut; British Crown assumes direct rule via 1858 Act. |
| **1885 (Dec)** | Nationalist Genesis | Foundation of Indian National Congress | W.C. Bonnerjee, A.O. Hume (Bombay) | First session attended by 72 delegates; inaugurated constitutional agitation. |
| **1905 (Oct)** | Mass Nationalism | Partition of Bengal & Swadeshi Movement | Lord Curzon, Lal-Bal-Pal, Tagore | Boycott of British cloth; National Council of Education; Vande Mataram. |
| **1909** | Communal Electorates | Morley-Minto Reforms | John Morley, Lord Minto II | Separate electorates for Muslims; official sanction of communalism. |
| **1919 (13 April)** | Colonial Barbarism | Jallianwala Bagh Massacre & Rowlatt Act | Brigadier-General Dyer, Mahatma Gandhi | Over 1,000 murdered at Amritsar; moral turning point of freedom struggle. |
| **1920 – 1922** | First Mass Movement | Non-Cooperation & Khilafat Movement | Mahatma Gandhi, Ali Brothers | Triple boycott of courts, colleges, and cloth; halted post Chauri Chaura. |
| **1929 (Dec)** | Sovereign Goal | Lahore Congress Session: Purna Swaraj | Jawaharlal Nehru (President) | Complete independence declared; 26 Jan 1930 fixed as First Independence Day. |
| **1930 (12 Mar – 6 Apr)**| Epic Mass Defiance | The Dandi March & Salt Satyagraha | Mahatma Gandhi & 78 satyagrahis | 240-mile march to Dandi; launched pan-Indian Civil Disobedience Movement. |
| **1932 (Sept)** | Social Integration | The Poona Pact | Mahatma Gandhi & Dr. B.R. Ambedkar | Abandoned separate electorates for Dalits; reserved 148 assembly seats. |
| **1935** | Constitutional Act | Government of India Act 1935 | British Parliament | Introduced Provincial Autonomy; blueprint for 1950 Indian Constitution. |
| **1942 (8 August)** | Final Mass Rebellion | The Quit India Movement ('Do or Die') | Mahatma Gandhi, AICC Bombay | Operation Zero Hour; underground networks; parallel governments established. |
| **1943 – 1945** | Armed Liberation | Indian National Army (INA) Campaigns | Netaji Subhas Chandra Bose | Provisional Government of Azad Hind; march to Kohima and Imphal. |
| **1946 (Feb–Aug)** | Imperial Collapse | RIN Naval Mutiny & Cabinet Mission | Naval ratings, Pethick-Lawrence, Nehru | Loyalty of armed forces broke; Direct Action Day triggered communal holocaust. |
| **1947 (15 August)** | Sovereign Liberation | Independence and Partition of India | Jawaharlal Nehru, Sardar Patel, Mountbatten | End of British Empire; creation of independent Dominions of India & Pakistan. |
