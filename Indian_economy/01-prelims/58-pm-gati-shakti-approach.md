## PM Gati Shakti Approach

- It is an approach for growth accelerating trustworthy infrastructure through synchronized, holistic, integrated and comprehensive planning based on knowledge, technology and innovation.

- Provides comprehensive database, Gati Shakti Digital Platform of ongoing & future projects of various ministries ie infrastructure ministries and ministries and states involved in development of Economic Zones. Ministries related to Social Infrastructure have also been integrated into PM Gati Shakti

- PM GatiShakti National Master Plan has been developed as a Digital Master Planning tool by BISAG-N (Bhaskaracharya National Institute for Space Applications and Geoinformatics) and has been prepared in dynamic Geographic Information System (GIS) platform wherein data on specific action plan of all the Ministries/Departments have been incorporated within a comprehensive database.

- **Note: **Logistics division has been transferred to Department for Promotion of Industry & Internal Trade under the Ministry of Commerce & Industry.

### **Objectives of PM Gati Shakti**

- Create next generation infrastructure by learning from past.

- Prepare an integrated plan for infrastructure for address missing gaps to ensure seamless movement of people, goods and services.

- Enhance ease of living, ease of doing business, minimise disruptions and expedite completion of works with cost efficiencies.

#### **Network Planning Group**

An Integrated Multimodal Network Planning Group (NPG) with heads of Network Planning Division of all connectivity infrastructure Ministries & Departments is responsible for unified planning and integration of the proposals and assist the EGOS in respect of its mandate.
