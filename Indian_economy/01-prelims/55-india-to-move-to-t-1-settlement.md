# India to move to T+1 Settlement

> **Source**: [India to move to T+1 Settlement](https://compass.rauias.com/economy/india-move-t1-settlement/)

---

*The settlement refers to the official transfer of shares from the seller's account to the buyer's account. Earlier, India was following T+2 settlement and has now adopted T+1 settlement.*

- T+1 means that trade-related settlements must be done within one day of the transaction's completion.

- Trades on Indian stock exchanges are currently settled in two working days after the transaction is completed (T+2).

- For example, if you buy shares on Wednesday, they will be credited to your Demat account by the next day, which is Thursday.

- Now, with the adoption of (T+1) settlement, the trade gets settled within one day.

- **Significance:**

- Most large stock markets, like in the US, Europe, Japan, still follow the T+2 settlement cycle of trade settlement.

- India has now become the second country after China to go for T+1 settlement.
