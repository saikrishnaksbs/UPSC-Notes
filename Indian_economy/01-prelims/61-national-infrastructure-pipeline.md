# National Infrastructure Pipeline

> **Source**: [National Infrastructure Pipeline](https://compass.rauias.com/economy/national-infrastructure-pipeline/)

---

- **Aim:** Envisages expenditure of around Rs 111 lakh crore over the next 5 years to realise $ 5 trillion economy.

- **Scope:** NIP was launched with 6,835 projects, which has expanded to over 9,000 projects covering 34 infrastructure sub-sectors

- **Sectors with highest Investment:** Energy (24%), Roads (19%), Urban Development (16%) and Railways (13%)

- **Funding:** Centre, states and the private sector in the proportion of 39:40:21.
