# Evolution of Planning In India

> **Source**: [Evolution of Planning In India](https://compass.rauias.com/economy/evolution-planning-in-india/)

---

India adopted Planning after its independence to promote holistic, comprehensive and integrated growth of the Indian Economy.

The Planning Commission was set up on the 15th of March 1950 through a Cabinet Resolution to formulate five year plan. The Planning Commission formulated twelve five year plans from 1950 to 2014.
